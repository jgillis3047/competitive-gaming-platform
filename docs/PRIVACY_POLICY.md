# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** March 19, 2025

This privacy policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use **Arena** (the "App").

## Information we collect

- **Account information:** Email, username, display name, and profile photo you provide when you register.
- **Usage data:** Challenges you create or join, game results, and in-app activity necessary to run the App (e.g. wallet balances, orders, notifications).
- **Device data:** We may collect device identifiers and push tokens to send you notifications (e.g. challenge updates, seller approval) if you allow them.

## How we use it

- To provide the App (challenges, feed, shop, wallet, seller dashboard).
- To process wallet top-ups and in-app purchases via Stripe.
- To send you transactional and optional push notifications.
- To improve the App and enforce our terms.

## Data sharing

- We use **Supabase** for authentication and database storage, and **Stripe** for payment processing. Each has its own privacy policy.
- We do not sell your personal data to third parties for advertising.

## Security

- We use industry-standard practices (HTTPS, secure auth, restricted access) to protect your data.

## Your rights

- You can update your profile and account settings in the App.
- You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at support@yourapp.com (replace with your actual support email before launch).

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.
